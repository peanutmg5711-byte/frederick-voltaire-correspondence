# 伏尔泰致腓特烈 1760.5.30 [D8947]

[Editorial comment: Tells him that Choiseul cannot act in accordance with his own views, but that France has no wish to see Frederick oppressed, that he should take care not to suffer a great defeat, and that France considered that he should keep Silesia.]
